# Fridge — real screen recording

Record the actual app at normal speed. Leave the five-second expiration intact.

## Set the scene

- A source window in the middle-left; a local Notes document or draft at the lower-left for the drop. Leave roughly 200 points clear on the right for magnets.
- Hide Fridge's welcome window with Keep running in menu bar.
- Use a sample document or chart without private information.
- For immediate delivery, uncheck Show Floating Thumbnail in ⌘⇧5 → Options. This is a macOS preference; the app does not change it for you.
- Record the whole display, including the right edge. Leave camera zooms and captions for editing.
- Current captures and magnet windows are visible to recording tools.

## One continuous take (about 30 seconds)

| Time | Action | Caption to add later |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Capture a small section with ⌘⇧4. | Take a screenshot. |
| 3–8 | Keep your pointer away. Let the preview disappear naturally. | Just passing through. |
| 8–12 | Capture again, then click the magnet before five seconds elapse. | Click to keep. |
| 12–18 | Move the pointer away and work briefly in the source window. The pinned capture stays. | Right where you left it. |
| 18–23 | Drag the screenshot body into a local draft. Do not send the draft. | Drag to share. |
| 23–26 | Click the magnet again to release it. | Done? Let it go. |
| 26–30 | Hold a clean desktop frame for the end card. | Fridge. Keep what sticks. |

Click the magnet itself to pin/release. Clicking the screenshot body previews it. Hover pauses the expiration timer, so keep the pointer away for the first expiration shot.

Capture two takes if possible. Keep a few seconds of clean footage before and after the action. The edited video can use a gentle push-in on the snap and follow the drag, while preserving actual app behavior.
